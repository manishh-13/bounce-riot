# Physical TV and phone acceptance checklist

Automated browser clients do not replace testing on the actual TV and phones. Use this checklist for the first house-party session.

## Before friends arrive
- Open the stable public game URL on the TV. Try Low graphics if the animated menu stutters.
- Use the TV's low-latency game mode if available. Disable motion interpolation if it adds visible delay.
- Put TV and phones on the same normal Wi-Fi, not an isolated guest network.
- Keep the TV page visible and choose Play (Host a party).
- Scan from one Android phone and one iPhone if available.
- Confirm the controller says DIRECT TO TV or CONNECTED VIA RELAY. Both are supported; direct is preferred.
- On Android, enter fullscreen. On iPhone, optionally launch from Add to Home Screen and rejoin.

## Control feel
- Move while holding the action button with the other thumb.
- Tap to hop; hold and release to slam. Confirm both gestures produce different moves.
- Move in all four directions and compare phone movement direction with the screen.
- Hold a charge, then open the controller menu. It must cancel instead of accidentally slamming.
- Exit fullscreen, return, and verify the controller requires a deliberate resume.
- Lock one phone, unlock it, and rejoin. The player number should stay the same while the seat is retained.
- Switch Wi-Fi briefly or walk out of range. Other players should remain able to play.

## Match quality
- Test two players first, then four, six and eight.
- Every person should be able to point to their character from the sofa.
- Players should be able to explain which wave knocked them off.
- No one should have to wait out the round after a knockout.
- The captain should be able to start, pause and rematch from the phone.
- Try all three arena variants and at least two rematches.

## Honest release criteria
This checklist has not been executed on your physical Android TV by the automated development environment. Record the TV model, browser version and phone/browser versions when reporting a problem. Do not claim a universal TV compatibility guarantee from successful laptop tests.
